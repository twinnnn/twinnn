About

Why this guide exists

Life Reset was created for people who are not lazy, but overloaded: too much noise, too much pressure, too little calm.

The idea

Most people already know the basics of what they should change: scroll less, sleep better, clean up their space, focus more, and stop overthinking everything. The problem is not knowledge. The problem is that most systems are too complicated.

This guide is intentionally simple: every day gives you one short lesson, one mental trick, and one small action. You do not need to fill out pages, track a hundred habits, or build a perfect morning routine.

Who is it for?

For people who want to collect themselves again: students, creators, young adults, busy people, or anyone who wants to rebuild structure in daily life.

Who is it not for?

It is not a replacement for medical or psychological treatment. This guide is for inspiration, structure, and self-management — not therapy.
